🚦 TRAFFIC SQUAD

Traffic Penalty Collection & Management

A digital platform to report traffic violations, upload proof, generate challans and manage penalty records.


Welcome to Traffic Squad

Traffic Squad is an online traffic violation management system. Police officers can register traffic cases with proof photographs. Vehicle owners can view their challans and penalty details online.

👤 User Portal

Register, log in and view traffic challans.

👮 Police Portal

Create cases and upload violation evidence.

📊 Admin Portal

Manage police accounts and daily reports.

📍 Traffic Information

Open Google Maps to view traffic conditions.

This is a college project prototype. Demo data is stored in your browser.

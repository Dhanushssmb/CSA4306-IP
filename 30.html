About Journal

The International Journal of Scientific and Social Excellence (IJSSE) provides a platform for researchers, academicians, scholars, and students to share their research findings and innovative ideas.

Our Mission

To encourage quality research and support the development and sharing of knowledge.

Our Scope

Scientific research, engineering, technology, management, education, social sciences, and interdisciplinary studies.

Publication Ethics

IJSSE expects authors, reviewers, and editors to follow ethical standards throughout the research and publication process.

Original Research

Authors must submit original work and properly acknowledge other sources.

Peer Review

Reviewers should provide fair, unbiased, and confidential feedback.

Editorial Integrity

Editors should make publication decisions based on research quality and journal policies.

Plagiarism

Submitted manuscripts should not contain copied content without proper citation.

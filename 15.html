How EasyToll Works

  1. Register and log in to your account.
  2. Add demo funds to your eWallet.
  3. Select a toll gate and purchase an advance eTicket, or apply for a toll pass.
  4. Show the generated QR code at the toll gate.
  5. The toll gate operator verifies the ticket or pass in the demonstration system.

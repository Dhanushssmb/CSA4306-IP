Animal Disease Information & Reports

These are sample educational entries, not diagnoses or prescriptions.

Foot-and-Mouth Disease

May involve fever, mouth lesions and lameness in susceptible livestock. Contact a veterinarian promptly.

Mastitis

Possible signs include a swollen udder, abnormal milk or pain. A veterinarian should assess the animal.

Parasitic Conditions

Possible signs vary and may include weight loss, poor condition or digestive problems. Seek professional diagnosis.

Respiratory Illness

Coughing, nasal discharge or breathing difficulty require veterinary assessment; severe breathing difficulty is urgent.
